Automatic router forwarding (UPnP) Asks your router to forward the port. UPnP must be enabled on the router.

Forward the port by hand

  1. Open your router page (often 192.168.0.1 or 192.168.1.1) and sign in.
  2. Find Port Forwarding, Virtual Server or NAT.
  3. Forward port 25565 (TCP and UDP) to this PC.
  4. Compare the router's WAN IP with your public IP above. If they differ, your provider uses CGNAT and forwarding won't work. Use playit.gg instead.
